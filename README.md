## 🚀 About Me
I'm a GRC and cybersecurity geek who loves building secure, compliant cloud environments on AWS. I get excited about automating security controls and translating complex regulations like SOC 2 , PCI -DSS into practical solutions that actually work. So technically, skilling towards GRC engineering.

My sweet spot is where security meets business operations - whether it's designing zero-trust architectures, streamlining product security processes, or making compliance less painful through automation. I speak both technical and business languages fluently.

When I'm not digging into compliance frameworks, securing cloud infrastructures or geeking out over python scripts, you'll find me exploring new tech tools, mentoring aspiring cybersecurity pros, or probably over-caffeinating while reading the latest security or compliance updates. I live for those "lightbulb moments" when security, compliance, and business goals all align perfectly.
